**F01: Index eligibility**
- **Evidence:** The supplied HTML for `https://acme.example/services/` contains `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow">`. The capture time, live status, headers, and redirect history are unknown.
- **Confidence:** High that the directive exists in the supplied source; unknown whether it is currently served. `follow` does not cancel `noindex`.
- **Action:** Check the current response and rendered HTML. If the directive is still present and the page is intended to be searchable, remove `noindex` at its source, such as the page or template setting. Also check for an `X-Robots-Tag` header or other applicable directives. Do not block the page in `robots.txt` as a substitute.
- **Acceptance check:** A fresh response for the intended final URL and its rendered HTML contain no applicable `noindex`; the response headers also contain no applicable `noindex`. Confirm that the relevant crawler can access the page. If Search Console access is available, separately inspect the URL and monitor its indexing status. Removing the directive makes indexing possible; it does not guarantee indexing or rankings.
- **Business rationale:** The stated goal is qualified consultation leads. Search eligibility is a prerequisite for this page to attract those leads through Google Search, but page demand, current organic traffic, and conversion contribution are unknown.
- **Measurement:** Baseline unknown. After any correction, compare page-level organic impressions and clicks in Search Console, and qualified organic consultation leads in analytics, using comparable periods where data is available. Review at 30, 60, and 90 days without assuming an uplift.
